Questions you might have

What is the catch?

Just the one, and it is a happy one. I would love to show your finished site in my portfolio and mention your group by name. That permission is really what I am being paid in. If it does not suit you, say so and we can talk it through.

Do we have to be a registered charity?

Not at all. Community groups, sports clubs, marae, school groups and small volunteer-run causes are all very welcome. If you are a business then this one is not for you, but do get in touch anyway and I will point you somewhere good.

Why can we not just use a free website builder?

You absolutely can, and for some groups that really is the right call. Free plans do tend to pop ads on your pages, hand you an address like yourgroup.builder.com, and get clunky once you add real content. If none of that bothers you, go for it with my blessing.

What happens if we need changes in a year?

Flick me an email and I will help where I can, still free, though I cannot promise a lightning turnaround. That is exactly why your site is built for you to edit and why you keep all the logins.

How many of these are you taking on?

A few at a time. Between study and work, I would much rather do two really well than five in a rush. If my hands are full I will let you know right away rather than leave you hanging.

Do you handle our donor or member data?

Nope, and that is very much on purpose. I will link your site up to whatever donation or membership tool you already use, but your supporter information stays entirely with you.

I would love to hear from you

Tell me what your group does, roughly how many people are involved, and whether you have a website at the moment.
